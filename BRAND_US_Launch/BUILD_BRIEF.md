### Regulatory and structural facts (verified October 2026)
- Tariffs: the Supreme Court struck down the IEEPA tariffs on February 20, 2026. The 10% Section 122 surcharge ran February 24 to July 24, 2026 and expired. A new Section 301 forced labor action took effect in late July 2026 adding 12.5% on all China origin goods, stacking on the existing China Section 301 list rates (List 3 is 25%) and on the MFN rate (2.6% for 8544.42.90, free for 8544.70.00). A second Section 301 investigation opened in March 2026 is still pending. Classification fight: 8544.42 (electric conductors fitted with connectors) versus 8544.70 (optical fiber cables). A CBP binding ruling decides the base rate; the 12.5% applies either way. Model duty as an input with scenarios, not a single number.
- De minimis is gone. Every China origin shipment needs formal entry. No parcel dropship model.
- Entity: Wyoming single member LLC, $100 to form, about $60 per year, no state income tax, strong privacy. Delaware is about $300 per year and only worth it for raising investment. BOI reporting is not required for US formed companies (FinCEN finalized the exemption August 11, 2026). A foreign owned single member LLC files Form 5472 with a pro forma 1120 every year; the penalty for missing it is $25,000. EIN by phone or fax with Form SS-4, no SSN needed. Form numbers and registration numbers keep their official format.
- US income tax: under the 1976 US Korea tax treaty, business profits are taxed only where the permanent establishment sits. Goods stored and shipped from FBA warehouses do not create a PE (Article 9(3)(b)). Keep no US office, no US employees, and no agent in the US who negotiates or signs contracts. If Mark becomes an owner of the LLC it becomes a partnership with Form 1065 and 37% withholding on her share of profit; the plan assumes Lucy owns 100% and Mark is paid under a written consulting or commission agreement. Note the alternative of Mark's own US company acting as distributor and importer of record with Lucy staying at the wholesale layer.
- Korea side: under the Foreign Exchange Transactions Act, Lucy must report overseas direct investment to her foreign exchange bank before capital moves (any stake of 10% or more, any amount), then file follow up reports (remittance, securities acquisition, annual business performance). Enforcement against individual online sellers is rising. Also flag the Korean overseas financial account report (KRW 500M month end aggregate threshold) for her 세무사.
- Trademark: separate from the entity. Foreign applicants must file through a US licensed attorney, about $350 per class plus attorney fees. Amazon Brand Registry accepts a pending application. Brand Registry gates A+ content, Brand Store, video ads, and hijacker protection.
- Amazon: Professional account, tax interview as a foreign owned entity, identity verification, USD bank account (Mercury or Relay for the LLC; Payoneer or WorldFirst as fallback). Product liability insurance of $1M is required once sales exceed $10K in a month. Amazon collects marketplace sales tax, but California may assert income tax nexus from FBA inventory; put it on the CPA list.
- Product compliance: the factory must be an HDMI Adopter with ATC test reports; HDMI LA runs seizures. Packaging and promotion may not use HDMI version numbers; use the cable category name (Ultra High Speed HDMI Cable). Listing titles may state 48Gbps and 8K. AOC heads contain active electronics, so obtain the factory's FCC test report. Add a California Prop 65 label. Get CL3 documentation before claiming in wall.
- Logistics: customs broker with a continuous bond (about $500 per year), binding ruling filed in week 1 (30 to 90 days), air DDP for the first 5 hero SKUs at 100 to 300 units each, FNSKU labels at the factory, third party QC before departure, 3PL for bulk inventory with FBA fed from it, ocean freight only on proven SKUs.
- Channels: Amazon US first. eBay as overflow at near zero incremental effort. Walmart Marketplace in Year 2. Temu only through its local seller program after the Amazon price floor is set; optional, not in the base case.
