# 11 Decision Log: open decisions, the defaults used, and what changes if they go the other way

Built October 8, 2026 from inputs/DECISIONS.md, build/BUILD_SPEC.md, build/figures.json (pulled from output/03_Financial_Model_5Y.xlsx) and build/facts.json. Every model number below is the Medium scenario at the delivered selector state (Scenario Medium, Duty Copper, FX 1,430) unless the row says otherwise. Model months: Month 1 is the first month with inventory live on Amazon (assumed early 2027). Status is Open on every row until Lucy or Mark writes the decision into inputs/DECISIONS.md and the build is rerun.

How to read the columns: Default used is what every file in output/ assumes today. What changes is quantified from the Sensitivity and Scenario Summary tabs of the workbook where the model can measure it, and labeled as a direction only where it cannot. By when is relative to the capital transfer (the Korean overseas direct investment report must be filed before capital moves) or to the launch week numbers in 07_90_Day_Launch_Plan.xlsx.

Acronyms used here: ODI (overseas direct investment report, 해외직접투자 신고), LLC (limited liability company), EIN (Employer Identification Number), CBP (US Customs and Border Protection), HTS (Harmonized Tariff Schedule), FOB (free on board, the price at the factory port), COGS (cost of goods sold), ASP (average selling price), FBA (Fulfillment by Amazon), TACOS (total advertising cost of sales), PE (permanent establishment), LOC (line of credit), SKU (stock keeping unit), FX (foreign exchange rate, KRW per USD), QC (quality control), 3PL (third party logistics warehouse), ATC (HDMI Authorized Test Center), FCC (Federal Communications Commission), CL3 (in wall cable fire rating), 세무사 (Korean licensed tax accountant).

## 4. How to record a decision

1. Edit the Default used cell in inputs/DECISIONS.md (or add a row for a D item).
2. For a model input (FX, duty scenario, ASP, ad phases, returns, cover months, PO terms), change the yellow input cell on the Assumptions tab of output/03_Financial_Model_5Y.xlsx or the matching value in build/build_model.py, then rerun build/build_model.py, the recalc, build/pull_figures.py and build/tie_out.py so every file picks up the new figures.
3. Change this row's Status from Open to Decided with the date and the name of the person who decided.
