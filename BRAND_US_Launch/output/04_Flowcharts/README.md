# 04_Flowcharts

Eight process charts for the BRAND US launch. Each chart is delivered as Mermaid source (.mmd), a PNG render (2400 px on the long side, white background) and a PDF. Rebuild with `python3 build/gen_flowcharts.py` then `render_mmd <file>.mmd` after `source build/env.sh`. Every chart carries a legend node that spells out its acronyms. Thresholds, weeks and owners come from BUILD_BRIEF.md sections 1 and 2 and BUILD_SPEC.md sections 2.6 to 2.8; no model figures are drawn here, so the charts do not change when the workbook is rerun.

| File | What it shows | Who uses it |
